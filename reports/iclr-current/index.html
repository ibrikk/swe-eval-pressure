Interpretive hierarchy.
The strongest claims are randomized same-task treatment effects with multiplicity correction. Process analyses are secondary/exploratory unless otherwise noted. Semantic-state-conditioned Said/Did analyses are descriptive because semantic state is post-treatment.

Research questions and design

RQ1. How frequently do predefined behaviors occur across models and conditions?
RQ2. Does pressure alter observable recognition, provenance assessment, resistance, or claimed response?
RQ3. Does pressure alter exploration, editing, verification, repair, resource use, or task performance?
RQ4. Does resource scarcity change execution volume and allocation across action classes?
RQ5. Are treatment effects moderated by placement or model?
RQ6. Are verbal responses consistent with objective same-task behavioral changes?
RQ7. Do headline behavioral effects recur in the independent rerun?

Treatment fidelity and outcome validity

Root-placement interpretation. Direct cue consumption is weakly observed for several passive-root conditions. Root-condition estimates therefore support an intention-to-treat effect of assignment; they do not by themselves establish that the model consciously read the pressure cue. Mechanism claims remain unresolved unless additional delivery evidence is recovered.

Limitations and interpretation boundaries

  • Passive/root treatment fidelity is incompletely observed; root-condition mechanism claims are therefore limited.
  • Semantic outcomes are generated by two LLM judges; concrete pressure/resource constructs have stronger reliability than generic evaluation recognition, and human validation remains pending.
  • Llama strict success is a capability floor and is not used for success-rate treatment inference.
  • Token and duration outcomes are heavy-tailed; robustness diagnostics accompany headline continuous effects.
  • The resource intervention is a textual scarcity claim rather than an externally enforced compute budget.
  • Cue-mechanism-specific comparisons have limited per-mechanism sample size.
  • The independent rerun is incomplete and remains descriptive.
